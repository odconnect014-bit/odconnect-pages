ODConnect

ODConnect is a privately operated integration tool that connects an operator's QuickBooks Online company to that operator's own bookkeeping records. It is not offered to the public, not listed in any app marketplace, and not available for use by anyone other than its operator.

What it does

Reads customers, items, classes, invoices, and payments from a QuickBooks Online company the operator has authorized, and creates invoice records in that company at the operator's direction. All processing happens on the operator's own computer.

Authorization

Access is granted by a QuickBooks Online administrator through Intuit's standard OAuth consent screen and can be revoked at any time from the Apps page inside QuickBooks Online or from the disconnect page.
